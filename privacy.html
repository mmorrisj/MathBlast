MathBlast — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

MathBlast collects no personal data, transmits nothing off your device, and contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking of any kind.

This policy covers the MathBlast game, whether played on the web or installed as an app from Google Play. It is short because there is very little to describe.

What is stored

MathBlast saves a small amount of information on the device you are playing on, using ordinary browser storage. That is:

  • The player names you type in, so more than one person can use the same device.
  • High scores, the wave reached, and the difficulty each run was played on.
  • Which maths facts you have answered, how quickly, and how often you got them wrong. This is what makes the game show you the questions you find hardest.
  • A record of practice grouped by topic — addition, fractions, percentages and so on — with a per-day roll-up of how much was played and how it went. This is what the in-game progress page shows a parent.
  • Your settings — difficulty, sound, reduced motion, colour-safe palette.

None of this is sent anywhere. It stays on the device, it is not readable by us or by anyone else, and it is not linked to any account, because there are no accounts.

What is not collected

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contact details.
  • No location data.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs or fingerprinting.
  • No usage analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • No photos, microphone or camera access — the game never asks for a permission.

No advertising and no third parties

There are no adverts in MathBlast and no advertising SDKs. There are no analytics or attribution SDKs. The game makes no network requests at all once it has loaded — it is designed to run entirely offline, and it does. There is no third party who receives anything, because nothing is sent.

Children

MathBlast is intended for children as well as adults, and is built to comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation including its provisions for children, and Google Play's Families policy. Since no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children either. There are no external links in the game, no social features, and no way to contact or be contacted by another player.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you remove it by removing it from your own device:

  • Installed app: uninstalling MathBlast, or clearing its storage in Android's app settings, deletes all of it.
  • In a browser: clearing site data for the page removes it.

There is no server-side copy to request, export or erase, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If MathBlast ever does start collecting or transmitting anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than only reflected in the text.
